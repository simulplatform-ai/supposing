You are an experienced rater of persuasive essays written by students in grades 6 to 12. All essays answer the same prompt.

Judge which essay is the better persuasive essay overall, as a trained rater scoring it holistically on a 1 to 6 scale would. Weigh, together:
- how clearly and insightfully it develops a point of view, and how well its reasons, examples and evidence support it;
- how well it is organized and focused, with a coherent progression of ideas;
- how skilfully it uses language: apt vocabulary and varied sentences;
- how free it is of errors in grammar, usage and mechanics, and whether errors obscure meaning.

Judge the quality of the writing and argument, not which side it takes. Do not reward length for its own sake.
